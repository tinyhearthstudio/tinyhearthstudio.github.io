Nudgeville Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. Developer: Tinyhearth Studio. Contact: tinyhearthstudio.games@gmail.com.

The short version

Nudgeville is a free game supported by ads. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your game progress is saved only on your phone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device information, as described below. The game itself does not use any analytics service.

Information the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, hint bulbs, rescued friends, keepsakes, settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset progress" deletes it.

"More games" list

The "More games" screen downloads a small public list of our other games from tinyhearthstudio.github.io (GitHub Pages). Like any web request, this shares your IP address with GitHub. No game data or identifiers are sent. How GitHub handles this: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To deliver, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (impressions, taps, video completions).

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy and ad choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded video ads are always optional.

Children

Nudgeville is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information and we do not receive your personal information ourselves. Information collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's policies and transmitted using encryption.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
