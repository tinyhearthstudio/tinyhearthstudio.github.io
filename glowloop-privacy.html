Glowloop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. Developer: Tinyhearth Studio. Contact: tinyhearthstudio.games@gmail.com.

The short version

Glowloop is a free game supported by ads. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your game progress is saved only on your phone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and anonymous game statistics and crash reports by Google Firebase. Both collect some device information, as described below.

Information the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, Firefly Book, Night Garden, streaks and settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes your progress but keeps your settings.

Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and make it better, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records how the app is used, such as levels started and finished, which bonus videos are chosen, and how often the app is opened, together with an app instance ID, device model, operating system, app version, language and approximate region. It does not record your name, email, contacts or precise location. We see these statistics only as totals and do not use them to identify you. How Google handles this data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can fix the bug. A report contains technical details only: what went wrong in the code, the device model, operating system, app version and an app instance ID. It does not contain your name, email, contacts or location.

Evening reminder (notifications)

If you agree, the game sets a gentle reminder for the evening. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself and work without the internet; no information about them is sent to us or to anyone else. Android asks for your permission first, and you can turn reminders off any time in the game's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Midnight fireflies

Midnight fireflies appear based on your phone's clock only. The game does not use or ask for your location.

Feedback and sharing

"Send feedback" in Settings opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. If you do, we receive your email address, what you write, and the details the game adds to help us: the app version, your phone model and Android version, the game language and the level you are on. We use them only to answer you and improve the game, and you can ask us to delete them at any time. "Share with friends" opens your phone's share menu with a link to the game; the game does not see who you share it with.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To deliver, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (impressions, taps, video completions) and diagnostic information.

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

"More games" list

To show our other games, the app downloads a small list from our website (tinyhearthstudio.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages). This request contains no personal information from the game. Like any website, GitHub may briefly log technical data such as your IP address for security; we do not receive or use it. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded video ads are always optional.

Children

Glowloop is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information, and apart from emails you choose to send us, we do not receive your personal information ourselves. Information collected by Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics is handled under Google's policies and transmitted using encryption.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
