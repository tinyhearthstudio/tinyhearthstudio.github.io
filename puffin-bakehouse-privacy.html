Puffin Bakehouse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Developer: Tinyhearth Studio. Contact: tinyhearthstudio.games@gmail.com.

The short version

Puffin Bakehouse is a free game supported by ads. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your game progress is saved only on your phone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads, as described below. We use Google Firebase to count how the game is played and to receive crash reports.

Information the game stores

Your progress (shops, coins, upgrades, settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset all progress" deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To deliver, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (impressions, taps, video completions).

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

App analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The game uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to understand how many people play, which features they use, and why the game crashes, so we can fix bugs and improve it. These tools may collect:

  • an app instance ID and your device's advertising ID (where allowed);
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version, country derived from your IP address);
  • game events, such as starting a session, opening a new shop or watching an optional video;
  • crash and error details.

We see this information only as statistics and crash reports, never linked to your name. Firebase data is kept for up to 14 months. Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the game schedules at most one notification a day on your phone (for example when your daily gift is ready, or a gentle note if you haven't visited your shop for a few days). Reminders are created on your device and need no account or server. You can turn them off any time in Settings in the game or in your phone's settings.

Ratings

Now and then, after you finish a shop, the game may show Google Play's own rating window. Your rating and review go directly to Google Play under Google's policies; the game does not see or store them.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded video ads are always optional.

Children

Puffin Bakehouse is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information and we do not receive your personal information ourselves. Information collected by Google AdMob and Firebase is handled under Google's policies and transmitted using encryption.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
