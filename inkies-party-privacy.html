Inkies Party Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026. Developer: Tinyhearth Studio. Contact: tinyhearthstudio.games@gmail.com.

The short version

Inkies Party is a free party game supported by ads. One to four people play together on the same phone. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no online multiplayer. Your game progress is saved only on your phone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device information, as described below. The game uses Google Firebase for basic, anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, as described below.

Information the game stores

Your progress (coins, hats, pens and paper styles you unlocked, solo ladder pages, daily challenge streak, your best Quick Draw time and your settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes it. Player names in the game are just "P1" to "P4"; the game never asks for anyone's name.

Reminders

If you say yes when the game asks, it schedules at most one reminder a day on your phone (for example when a new daily challenge is ready). Reminders are created on the device, not sent from a server. You can turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in your phone's notification settings.

Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count anonymous game events (for example "round finished" or "match finished") and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash and error reports. These can include an app instance ID, device model, operating system version, app version and the time of the event. They do not include names, contacts or anything you type. We use them only to fix bugs and improve the game. How Google handles this: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

"More games" list

The "More games" screen downloads a small public list of our other games from tinyhearthstudio.github.io (GitHub Pages). Like any web request, this shares your IP address with GitHub. No game data or identifiers are sent. How GitHub handles this: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner ads on its menu screens, an occasional interstitial ad between matches (never during a match), and optional rewarded video ads, through Google AdMob. To deliver, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (impressions, taps, video completions).

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded video ads are always optional.

Children

Inkies Party is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information and we do not receive your personal information ourselves. Information collected by Google AdMob and Firebase is handled under Google's policies and transmitted using encryption.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
