Reefbloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026. Developer: Tinyhearth Studio. Contact: tinyhearthstudio.games@gmail.com.

The short version

Reefbloom is a free game supported by ads. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your game progress is saved only on your phone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and anonymous game statistics and crash reports by Google Firebase. Both collect some device information, as described below.

Information the game stores

Your progress (levels, coins, boosters, reef, settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset progress" deletes it.

Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and make it better, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records how the app is used, such as levels started and finished, which bonus videos are chosen, and how often the app is opened, together with an app instance ID, device model, operating system, app version, language and approximate region. It does not record your name, email, contacts or precise location. We see these statistics only as totals and do not use them to identify you. How Google handles this data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can fix the bug. A report contains technical details only: what went wrong in the code, the device model, operating system, app version and an app instance ID. It does not contain your name, email, contacts or location.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the game schedules at most one notification a day on your phone (for example when your daily gift is ready), never between 9 pm and 9 am. Reminders are created on your device and need no account or server. You can turn them off any time in Settings in the game or in your phone's settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To deliver, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (impressions, taps, video completions).

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded video ads are always optional.

Children

Reefbloom is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information and we do not receive your personal information ourselves. Information collected by Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics is handled under Google's policies and transmitted using encryption.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
